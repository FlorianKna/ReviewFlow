Why it matters for Google

Reviews aren't just trust signals. They're ranking signals.

Local search results run on three things: relevance, distance, and prominence. Reviews feed directly into the third one, and replies feed the trust that turns a search into a call.

1

Relevance

How closely your profile matches what someone actually searched for.

2

Distance

How close your business is to the person doing the searching.

3

Prominence

How well-known and trusted Google judges your business to be, built largely through reviews.

What actually moves it: a steady stream of recent reviews beats a pile of old ones from years back, higher average ratings improve click-through, and reviews that naturally mention what you do help Google understand what you're known for. Replying to every review, quickly, signals an active business, builds trust with the next customer reading it, and tends to encourage more people to leave one in the first place.

FAQ

Good questions

Do I need to give you my Google password?

No. You add me as a "Manager" on your Business Profile, which lets me reply to reviews and edit basic info. I can never remove your listing, manage other users, or access your Google account itself.

Are the replies AI-written?

Drafted with AI, always. Posted without a human reading it first, never. Every single reply is checked by me before it goes live under your name.

What if I don't want to grant Manager access?

That's fine. I can send you the drafted, reviewed replies each month by email instead, and you paste them in yourself. A little more work on your side, same quality of writing.

Is my data safe?

I only access what's needed to reply to reviews on your behalf, nothing more. Your data isn't sold or shared with anyone.

Can I cancel anytime?
