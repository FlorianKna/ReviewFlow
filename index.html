FAQ

Good questions

Do I need to give you my Google password?

No. You add me as a "Manager" on your Business Profile, which lets me reply to reviews and edit basic info. I can never remove your listing, manage other users, or access your Google account itself.

Are the replies AI-written?

Drafted with AI, always. Posted without a human reading it first, never. Every single reply is checked by me before it goes live under your name.

What if I don't want to grant Manager access?

That's fine. I can send you the drafted, reviewed replies each month by email instead, and you paste them in yourself. A little more work on your side, same quality of writing.

Is my data safe?

I only access what's needed to reply to reviews on your behalf, nothing more. Your data isn't sold or shared with anyone.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, including during the free month. No contracts, no lock-in.
